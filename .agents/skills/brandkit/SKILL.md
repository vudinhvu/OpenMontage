# Brand Kit for OpenMontage

Use when an approved brand kit or explicit brand direction is provided.

1. Carry approved palette, typography, logo clearspace, and tone into the selected playbook or project design tokens.
2. Do not invent brand values. Missing tokens stay unverified and require user-provided direction.
3. Validate contrast, safe zones, and logo visibility in final review.
