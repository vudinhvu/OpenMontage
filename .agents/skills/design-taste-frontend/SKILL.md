# Design Taste for OpenMontage Motion

Use during scene planning and renderer authoring for designed rather than default-looking motion.

1. Establish a deliberate visual system before scene implementation: hierarchy, density, palette, type scale, grid, and motion vocabulary.
2. Give each scene a distinct narrative job. Avoid repeating generic cards or identical transitions.
3. Prefer restrained, readable motion that supports narration; final review must check legibility and visual variety.
